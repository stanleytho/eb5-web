About Us

BD Ventures LLC is a Pacific Northwest company that brings a better approach to multifamily and commercial construction. We combine deep product knowledge, direct sourcing, and hands-on project support, and we bring the same execution-first mindset to the Veneta Apartments EB-5 opportunity.

Who We Are
  • Our commercial brand, Vuagg, supplies coordinated interior finishes — flooring, cabinetry, countertops, and tile — for multifamily, hospitality, and commercial projects
  • Direct relationships with trusted manufacturers, a local warehouse, and a dedicated showroom
  • Projects across the Western United States: Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Utah, California, and Nevada
  • Our mission: dependable materials, streamlined coordination, and service that helps partners build with confidence
Our Role in Veneta Apartments
  • Veneta Apartments is a 128-unit multifamily community in Lane County, Oregon, developed by HT Investment Properties & Affiliates (developing since 1998)
  • The project is sponsored by the Zephyrus Regional Center, approved for Lane County
  • The BD Ventures team works with Hirson EB-5 to guide investors through the project details, eligibility, and Source of Funds requirements
Our Values
  • Integrity: We do what we say we will do and build trust through honest communication.
  • Quality: We partner with those who share our commitment to quality and durability.
  • Partnership: We succeed when our partners succeed. Your goals are our top priority.
  • Accountability: We take ownership of our work and deliver reliable solutions you can count on.
  • Continuous Improvement: We are always looking for better products, better processes, and better outcomes.
