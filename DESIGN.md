## Overview
Decision by the owner: keep the brochure's visual identity, turn the print pages into a website. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Vietnamese investors and agents. Brand shown: BD Ventures.

## Colors
Navy carries the bar, cover gradient (160deg navy → navy-2 → navy) and closing section. Gold is the accent: kickers, dividers, bullets, buttons, Part numbers. Ivory and tint alternate as section grounds. Gold is only used as text on navy; on light grounds use `gold-on-light` (contrast) or navy.

## Typography
Playfair Display for headings and figures, Inter for text and small tracked labels. Both load from Google Fonts and support Vietnamese.

## Layout
1040px content width. Sticky navy bar plus a section nav row (current section underlined in gold). Sections alternate ivory / white. Mobile: nav row scrolls sideways, two-column blocks stack, tables scroll.

## Components
Part header (navy square number + serif title), section heading with hairline, lettered-free gold-dash bullets, metric cards, navy lead box with gold edge, ivory callouts, navy-header tables, navy timeline circles, capital-stack bar, gold buttons (navy text), dark contact section with form card.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep the brochure palette; add no new hues.
- Do keep disclaimers visible in the flow.
- Don't put gold text on white or ivory.
- Don't invent photos; add real renderings when supplied.
