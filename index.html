Part 03

The EB-5 Program — How It Works

The EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program, administered by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), allows qualified foreign nationals to obtain U.S. permanent residency (a "Green Card") by investing capital that creates American jobs.

U.S. Immigration and Nationality Act § 203(b)(5)
Program Requirements
  • Minimum Investment: $800,000 in a Targeted Employment Area (TEA) such as a rural area — Veneta qualifies
  • Standard Investment: $1,050,000 in non-TEA urban areas
  • Job Creation: Each investor's capital must support the creation of at least 10 full-time U.S. jobs
  • New Commercial Enterprise: Investment must be in a new or restructured U.S. business
  • At-Risk Capital: Funds must be committed to the project and at risk throughout the investment period
  • Regional Center Path: Investors in USCIS-designated regional centers benefit from "indirect job counting" — construction and economic multiplier jobs all qualify
  • Zephyrus RC: The sponsoring regional center is fully approved and covers Lane County, Oregon
  • Conditional Green Card: Initially granted for 2 years (I-526E approval + visa issuance)
  • Permanent Green Card: Conditions removed by filing I-829 when investment and job requirements are met
Key Process Steps
1

I-956F Project Application Filed

Regional Center files Form I-956F with USCIS to qualify the Veneta project. Investors may file I-526E petitions ten days after I-956F is filed (or upon receipt issuance). You can begin preparing your Source of Funds documentation immediately.

2

Investor Files I-526E Petition

Each investor submits an I-526E petition demonstrating lawful source of funds, investment capital, and eligibility. USCIS reviews and approves the petition. During this phase, Source of Funds documentation is prepared and compiled.

3

Visa Issuance — Conditional Green Card

Upon I-526E approval, investors residing abroad attend a consular interview. Investors already in the U.S. may apply for adjustment of status. A 2-year conditional permanent resident card is issued to the investor and qualifying family members (spouse, children under 21).

4

I-829 — Conditions Removed (Permanent Residency)

Within 90 days before the conditional green card expires, investors file Form I-829 to remove conditions. Upon approval, the investor and family receive unconditional U.S. permanent residency. Capital is returned per the fund agreement terms.

Vietnamese investors may begin gathering Source of Funds documentation immediately — this is typically the most time-consuming step. Working with a qualified immigration attorney early significantly reduces delays and strengthens the petition.

Part 04

Benefits for Vietnamese Investors

Vietnam is among the fastest-growing EB-5 investor communities in the United States. Vietnamese investors are attracted by strong U.S. educational institutions, stable property-backed investments, and the ability to establish a family legacy in America. The EB-5 program uniquely serves this goal while preserving capital through a structured loan investment.

Immigration Benefits

U.S. Green Card for the Whole Family

The investor, their spouse, and all unmarried children under age 21 receive conditional U.S. permanent residency. Family members gain the right to live, work, and study anywhere in the United States — without employer sponsorship or school-specific visas.

World-Class U.S. Education Access

Children of Green Card holders qualify for in-state university tuition rates — saving tens of thousands of dollars versus international student fees. Access to top American universities, scholarships, and financial aid becomes immediately available.

Healthcare & Quality of Life

Permanent residents access U.S. healthcare programs and can benefit from one of the highest-quality healthcare systems in the world. Families gain long-term stability and security in a politically stable environment.

Capital Preservation — Real Estate Backed

Unlike direct equity in a startup, this EB-5 investment is structured as a loan to the project — backed by a tangible 128-unit multifamily asset in a growing U.S. market. The investment is not a donation; capital return is structured at the conclusion of the loan term.

No Language or Business Requirements

Unlike the E-2 or L-1 visa, EB-5 does not require active management of a business, English proficiency, or ongoing employment in the U.S. The investor simply meets the capital and job creation requirements through the regional center structure.

Job Creation — EB-5 Compliance

The core requirement of the EB-5 program is that each investor's capital creates or preserves at least 10 full-time U.S. jobs. Through the regional center indirect job counting methodology, construction projects typically generate substantial job creation well beyond the minimum threshold — providing a critical buffer for investor eligibility.

USCIS EB-5 Regional Center — Indirect Job Counting
How Job Creation Is Counted
  • Direct Jobs: On-site construction workers and permanent property management staff employed by the project entity
  • Indirect Jobs: Workers employed by suppliers and contractors throughout the supply chain (lumber, concrete, roofing, plumbing, electrical, appliances, etc.)
  • Induced Jobs: Jobs created in the local economy as workers spend wages at local businesses, restaurants, and services
  • Regional center investors benefit from all three categories — significantly expanding eligible job counts compared to direct EB-5
